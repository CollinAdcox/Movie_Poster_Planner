# Movie Poster Planner

My app helps movie executives decide the best poster style for their movie by looking at correlations between movie style, genre, earnings, and reviews.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
 My user would be movie executives. They have to figure out what is the best approach to creating a poster to advertise a movie. Different aspects and features of posters give customers different ideas and expectations for what they movie will be, so they must find the best way to portray the story they want customers to see. Without my app, it is difficult know how customers view different posters and how it relates to overall earnings.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Sub-question 1:** **Customer perspective** Is there a correlation between IMDB scores and earnings?
2. **Sub-question 2:** **AI Description** Can we have AI read and give descriptions between different types of posters for easy comparison?
3. **Sub-question 3:** **Poster performance** Do certain styles of posters correlate to certain earnings thresholds

## Data
The [Movies Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/harshitshankhdhar/imdb-dataset-of-top-1000-movies-and-tv-shows)) on Kaggle, collected in 2020. The dataset is shared under public domain by its creator.
